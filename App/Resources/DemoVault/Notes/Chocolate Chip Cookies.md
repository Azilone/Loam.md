# Chocolate Chip Cookies

> Best while still warm. Let the dough rest **at least 30 minutes**.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Note |
|:-----------|-------:|:-----|
| Flour | 250 g | all-purpose |
| Soft butter | 125 g | |
| Brown sugar | 120 g | |
| White sugar | 80 g | |
| Egg | 1 | room temperature |
| Chocolate chips | 200 g | dark preferably |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp | |
| Salt | 1 pinch | |

## Steps

1. Beat the butter and sugars until creamy.
2. Add the egg, then mix.
3. Fold in the flour, baking powder and salt.
4. Add the chips, cover, and chill for 30 min.
5. Shape walnut-sized balls, 5 cm apart.
6. Bake 10 to 12 min at 180 °C, *until the edges turn golden*.

## Tips
- A pinch of flaky salt on each ball before baking.
- ==Don't overbake==: cookies firm up as they cool.
