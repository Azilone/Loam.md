# Tips for Better Sleep

> Sleep is prepared during the day, not only in the evening.

## The basics
1. Same bedtime and wake-up time, **7 days a week**.
2. No screens 30 minutes before bed.
3. A cool (17 to 19 °C) and dark bedroom.
4. No caffeine after 2 pm.

## To remember
- ==Morning light== helps set the internal clock.
- Alcohol fragments sleep, even in small amounts.
