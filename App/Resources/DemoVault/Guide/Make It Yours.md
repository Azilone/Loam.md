# Make It Yours

Loam starts small on purpose. Everything beyond writing is a **plugin**: you keep the ones you use and switch off the others.

## Plugins

Open **Settings > Plugins**. Search, tasks, daily notes, mind maps, boards, habits, meetings, the graph: each one is a plugin with its own settings. Turn one off and it disappears from the app; your notes do not change.

## Look and feel

**Settings > Appearance** has themes and fonts. **Settings > Sidebars** chooses what the sidebars show.

## Bring your notes

Come from Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Craft, Bear or Evernote? Run **Import from Another App…** in `⌘P`. Your agent moves the notes into a new vault, and nothing is lost on the way: a note is a `.md` file.

## Use all your computers

Open **Settings > Sync** to keep this vault the same on all your Macs.

## Your notes are yours

- A note is a plain Markdown file in a folder. Open it in any editor, back it up like any folder.
- Properties live in the YAML frontmatter at the top of the note.
- Tasks, collections, mind maps and boards are files too (`.base`, `.canvas`, Markdown).

## When you are ready

- Replace these demo notes: delete the folders you do not need, or ask Loam to remove the demo.
- Write your own plugin: `loam guide plugins` in a terminal.
