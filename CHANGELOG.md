# Changelog

What changed in Loam for the people who use it. The first section with items becomes the notes of the next release:
each beta shows everything since the last stable version, and the stable release closes the section under its version.
How to write it: AGENTS.md, "Releasing an update".

## Unreleased

- Fixed: a click in the empty space after a link opens nothing; only a click on the link itself opens it.
- Choose where daily notes go with your own date pattern: `yyyy/MM MMMM`, `yyyy-MM`, or leave it empty for none.
- The daily notes settings show their subfolder patterns as chips you tap, and list the journal oldest first if you turn "Newest first" off.
- Daily notes read newest first in the Files panel: the latest day, month and year sit at the top of the Journal folder.

## 0.1.2 - 2026-11-07

- Fixed: a board or collection inside a note follows a theme change (light, dark or another theme) at once, card titles included.
- Reorder a board's columns: drag a column by its header.
- Collections are folders: click one to open it, make one from New, or embed one in a note.
- Daily notes go to a Journal folder, sorted by year and month.
- When an update is ready, "Update Loam" appears in the vault menu: one click restarts into the new version.
- Fixed: the Home page stayed blank after closing the last tab with ⌘W.
- Fixed: the sidebar could scroll into empty space after folding a section.
